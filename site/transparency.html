Economics

Miner fees are the only protocol-level cost.

No protocol fee

No treasury, royalty, auction, token emission, burn, or reward is encoded.

No market metric

Success is completion, comprehension, repeat use, creation, sharing, cost, and deterministic state, not volume or floor price.

Service disclosure

A coordinator or creator may charge separately. Such fees are optional commercial terms, not Bitcoin-enforced protocol rights.

Kill criteria

The protocol must stop when evidence says stop.

  • Any wallet path can steal funds or substitute an output.
  • Target devices cannot clearly display or contribute safe signatures.
  • Users misunderstand permanent linkage or identity claims after onboarding.
  • Independent indexers cannot agree.
  • Common relay policy rejects the canonical form materially.
  • Session abandonment, Sybil concentration, fees, or low repeat use exceed published gates.
  • Users cannot distinguish the behavior from prior ChainBloom semantics.
