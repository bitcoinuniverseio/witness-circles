Review baseline

Final protocol. Public Bitcoin. User-controlled keys.

The repository includes jurisdiction-neutral legal review materials for qualified counsel. This page is a plain-language risk notice, not legal terms or legal advice.

Non-custodial

The protocol and static site do not hold private keys or recover lost keys. Coordinators should accept only public transaction data and signature contributions.

Public data

Inputs, output scripts, amounts, fees, block positions, and same-key lineages are public and cannot be erased from Bitcoin by an application operator.

Protocol status

Wallet, parser, indexer, dependency, relay, reorganization, metadata, and service defects can cause loss, delay, privacy harm, or incorrect presentation.

No financial claim

The final protocol creates no asset, token, yield, royalty, market, supply, price, guaranteed value, or future return.

No identity claim

Keys are not people. A Circle is not attendance, membership, endorsement, credential, title, or legal signature status.

Content

Optional metadata must respect rights, consent, privacy, and applicable law. Services can moderate presentation without changing Bitcoin history.

Before publication

Qualified review is required.

An actual operator must add identity, jurisdictions, effective dates, support and privacy contacts, vendors, data flows, retention, consumer rights, cookie behavior, governing law, dispute process, accessibility process, and region-specific obligations.

Do not treat generated baseline materials as binding terms. Counsel must review consumer, privacy, sanctions, content, electronic-signature, financial-services, tax, intellectual-property, and platform obligations.
