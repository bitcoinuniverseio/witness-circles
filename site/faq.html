Direct answers

Questions worth asking before you sign.

Is this a token or NFT?

No. The final protocol creates no transferable asset, supply, balance, royalty, or market entitlement.

Does it prove attendance?

No. It proves exact output-key authorization. A key can be remote, shared, automated, sold, or controlled by the same actor as another key.

Can the coordinator steal?

A coordinator can stall or censor. It cannot redirect a signature from a correct wallet because permitted signatures commit to every input and output.

Why reuse the same key?

Exact script reuse makes continuation deterministic. It also creates public linkage, so this is not a privacy feature.

Can I transfer a lineage?

No protocol transfer exists. Spending normally moves the Bitcoin but closes the lineage.

Can I sell it?

There is no safe listing operation. Off-platform key sales remain possible and risky, but do not create an authoritative protocol ownership claim.

Where is the description?

Only its SHA-256 hash is in Bitcoin. Preserve the canonical manifest privately or through optional mirrors.

What if fees rise?

Wait or leave. Your wallet should reject a fee above your absolute or rate cap. Honest invitations do not punish you for declining.

Can I bump the fee?

Coordinated RBF requires every participant to sign a new transaction. CPFP is not a protocol operation.

Is mainnet ready?

The grammar uses current Bitcoin primitives, but this release does not claim production readiness or enabled mainnet creation.
