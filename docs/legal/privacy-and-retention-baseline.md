# Privacy and Retention Baseline for Legal Review

**Requires counsel and privacy review. Actual data mapping is required.**

## Public Bitcoin data

Circle transactions, input and output scripts, amounts, fees, block positions, and lineage relationships are public Bitcoin data. Same-key successors and co-spends create intentional linkability that an operator cannot erase from Bitcoin.

## Optional service data

A coordinator may process invitations, capabilities, IP/security logs, manifest content, profile preferences, notification settings, support messages, and aggregate analytics. The final notice must state exact purposes, legal bases, vendors, regions, retention periods, rights, and contact information.

## Minimization

Do not collect seeds, private keys, complete signing secrets, unrelated wallet balances, cross-site advertising identifiers, or address clusters unrelated to the selected Circle. Use aggregate analytics where possible and honor opt-out controls.

## Retention baseline

- Expired invitation capabilities: delete promptly after operational and abuse-review needs end
- Security logs: retain only for a documented bounded period
- Optional manifests: retain under user-visible hosting policy
- Account/profile data: delete or export under reviewed user-rights process
- Canonical Bitcoin-derived data: may be re-derived and is publicly available

## Cookies

The static reference site needs no advertising or behavioral cookie. Any deployed authentication or analytics cookie requires a reviewed notice and consent model where applicable.
