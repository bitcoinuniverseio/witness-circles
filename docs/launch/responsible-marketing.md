# Responsible Marketing Policy

## Required claims discipline

Every claim must be reproducible from the specification, Bitcoin data, a released build, or published measurement. Clearly label draft, experimental, Signet-only, simulated, or application-level behavior.

Never claim:

- Guaranteed adoption, profit, value, scarcity, or returns
- Official Bitcoin endorsement
- Identity, attendance, or personhood proof
- Privacy or anonymity
- Decentralization for coordinator or metadata services that are operated centrally
- Mainnet safety before the production gates pass
- A live statistic that is simulated, cached without disclosure, or unavailable

## Ethical urgency

Real invitation expiry, limited pilot support, and scheduled community events may be stated exactly. Do not invent scarcity, fake online users, hidden queues, loss-framed streaks, or repeated prompts after dismissal.

## Sustainable engagement

Encourage meaningful collaboration, creation, learning, transparent milestones, personal history, and optional notifications. Do not use rewards tied to volume, fee spending, referrals, wealth, or speculative price.

## Review

Technical, security, privacy, accessibility, and legal reviewers approve launch claims within their areas. Marketing copy cannot override their findings.
