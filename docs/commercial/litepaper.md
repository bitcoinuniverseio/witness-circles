# Witness Circles Litepaper

## Sign one Bitcoin moment together

A Circle is a Bitcoin transaction everyone can inspect. Each participant contributes a small dedicated Taproot input, signs the same complete transaction, and receives the value back to the same key minus an equal share of the miner fee.

After confirmation, an explorer draws the moment as a constellation. Later Circles can extend each participant's path.

## What makes it different

- The group action and the proof are the same Bitcoin transaction.
- No issuer decides who participated.
- No token, market, reward, or promised value is created.
- Anyone can build a compatible parser or explorer.
- Optional descriptions can be private while their exact hash remains committed.

## What it does not prove

A Circle does not identify a person, certify attendance, or make a key permanent. It intentionally links participant inputs in public, so dedicated UTXOs and informed consent are essential.

## Who it is for

Open-source teams, creators, communities, families, educators, and Bitcoin developers can mark real collaborative moments. Spectators can explore and verify without a wallet.

## Current status

Version 1 is an experimental regtest and Signet-ready specification and reference package. Mainnet creation remains subject to wallet, privacy, security, and interoperability gates.
