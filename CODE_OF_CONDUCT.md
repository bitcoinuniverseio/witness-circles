# Community Conduct

Participation should be technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe.

Expected conduct includes respectful disagreement, evidence-backed claims, disclosure of conflicts, care with security information, and respect for user privacy. Harassment, doxxing, threats, deceptive promotion, fabricated activity, financial manipulation, and publication of unpatched fund-loss exploits are unacceptable.

Moderation decisions should be documented, proportionate, and appealable. Protocol facts remain derivable from Bitcoin even when optional community content is moderated.
